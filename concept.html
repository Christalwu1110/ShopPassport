01 — Stamp system

The Bristol Series.
Every stamp a small artwork.

One shared format: scalloped perforation, tinted frame, a rich illustrated scene and a bold slab-serif label. Each shop gets its own palette, and the series still reads as one family.

02 — Collection states

Four states.
Read at a glance, no labels needed.

Collected

Full colour, cancelled with a dated postmark from the shop.

Not collected

A faded ghost with a lock. Visible, so curiosity does the work.

Limited edition

Holographic foil that shifts as you tilt the phone. Numbered.

Achievement

A wax seal for finished routes and completed series.

04 — Prompts

Copy, paste, generate.
Tuned to this look.

Written for Midjourney. For other tools, drop the --ar parameter. Colour is left to the model: the prompts describe mood and style, not hex codes.

05 — Tips

Keep the set consistent.

Stamps first

Run prompt 01. When the illustration style is right, reuse it on every screen with --sref or a fixed --seed.

Three-phone shots

Ask for "three iPhone mockups on a soft light canvas". It reads like a real Dribbble shot.

Avoid sepia

Append --no beige, sepia, vintage paper to keep it bright and modern.

Freedom dial

--stylize 250 is more artistic. Lower it for tighter, more literal UI.
